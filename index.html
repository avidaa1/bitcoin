Quantum Risk Assessment
Timeline to Threat
No CRQC exists (Jul 2026): Breaking secp256k1 ECDSA needs ~1,200–2,330 logical qubits. Best 2026 machines: ~1,000–1,500 physical / ~100 logical qubits — a 400–500× gap.

Exposed supply: Over 34% of circulating BTC has exposed public keys on-chain (>$700B value) via address reuse or legacy P2PK. Includes Satoshi's ~1.1M BTC.

Protection: BIP-360 (post-quantum ML-DSA addresses) merged Feb 2026, now on testnet. BIP-361 tracks migration.

Real risk today instead: Weak-entropy wallets (Milk Sad/CVE-2023-39910) — 120,000+ wallets brute-forceable in ~4 sec on GPU. Not a Bitcoin flaw — a bad-RNG flaw in specific old tools.

Verdict: Quantum threat is bounded, 5–20 year horizon (Adam Back: 20–40yr; Google migration goal: within a decade). Not exploitable today.

The Epstein–MIT–Bitcoin Core Funding Connection — Documented Facts

This is one of the most widely misunderstood stories in Bitcoin's history — mostly because the timeline gets flattened. Here is the actual sequence, sourced, with the critical distinction upfront:

Bitcoin was NOT funded by Epstein. Bitcoin launched January 3, 2009, built by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto with no known outside funding. Epstein's documented MIT donations began around 2012–2013 — 3–4+ years after Bitcoin already existed. What actually happened: Epstein money later flowed through an MIT institute into salaries for some already-established Bitcoin Core maintainers. That is a real, documented, serious story — but it is a story about post-creation development funding, not Bitcoin's origin.

The Moving Gears — What Actually Makes BTC Rise or Fall

Eight mechanisms, ranked by how directly and how often each one has moved price historically. Most large moves are two or more of these firing at once, not a single cause.

1. Spot ETF FlowsVery High
Mechanism: ETFs must buy/sell real BTC to match share creation/redemption. A $500M single-day outflow forces real sell pressure on spot markets. Nov 2025: -$3.48B outflows coincided with BTC falling from $90K-range into the $70Ks within weeks.
2. Federal Reserve Policy & Global LiquidityVery High
Mechanism: Bitcoin trades as a liquidity-sensitive risk asset in the short-to-medium term (30-day correlation with S&P 500 has hit 0.74+ during hawkish stretches). Rate holds/hikes tighten dollar liquidity globally, pulling capital out of speculative assets including BTC.
3. Halving Supply ShockHigh
Mechanism: New issuance is cut in half roughly every 4 years (most recently Apr 2024, 6.25→3.125 BTC/block). Same demand chasing half the new supply, mechanically, historically preceded a rally 12-18 months later in all 4 cycles so far — though each cycle's % gain has been smaller (diminishing returns, see patterns below).
4. Corporate Treasury & Sovereign AccumulationHigh
Mechanism: Strategy alone has bought tens of thousands of BTC in single quarters, funded by convertible debt/equity issuance — real buy pressure independent of retail sentiment. US Strategic Reserve (~328K BTC) and other sovereigns permanently remove supply from circulation.
5. Regulatory & Executive Policy ShiftsHigh
Mechanism: Binary legal-status changes reprice risk instantly. Jan 2024 spot ETF approval: $46K→$73K in 2 months. Trump's Jan/Mar 2025 executive orders (Strategic Reserve, anti-CBDC stance, GENIUS Act) shifted sentiment bullish, though October 2025's tariff-threat shock erased $19B in leveraged positions in a single day — proving policy risk cuts both ways.
6. Leverage & Liquidation CascadesMedium-High
Mechanism: Futures open interest can exceed $30B+; when price crosses a cluster of leveraged positions' liquidation price, forced selling (or buying, on short squeezes) accelerates the move mechanically — not driven by new information, just cascading margin calls.
7. Tether (USDT) Issuance & Stablecoin SupplyMedium-High
Mechanism: New stablecoin minting is dry powder waiting to buy — historically stablecoin supply growth has led BTC price moves by 2-4 weeks, since minted USDT typically gets deployed into crypto markets shortly after.
8. Geopolitical Shocks & SanctionsMedium
Mechanism: Sanctions-evasion demand (Iran/Hormuz tolls), war/conflict flight-to-neutral-asset narratives, and OFAC enforcement actions create real but smaller, more localized demand/supply shifts — rarely the primary driver, but a real contributing input.

Undeniable Patterns in Bitcoin's History
4-Year Halving Cycle4 for 4
Every halving to date has been followed by a new ATH within 12-18 months. No exceptions across 4 cycles (2012, 2016, 2020, 2024).
Diminishing Returns LawConsistent decline
Cycle 1: +9,335% · Cycle 2: +2,944% · Cycle 3: +693% · Cycle 4: +98–100%. Each cycle's percentage gain has been smaller than the last, every time, as market cap grows.
MVRV Z-Score Cycle SignatureRepeats every cycle
Every major cycle top has coincided with MVRV Z-Score entering the red zone (>6-7); every major bottom has coincided with it going negative or near-zero. Held true in 2013, 2017, 2021, and the 2022 bottom.
Fear & Greed at ExtremesReliable contrarian signal

August Seasonal Weakness4 straight red Augusts
August has closed negative for 4 consecutive years running, averaging roughly -10%, regardless of the month's starting momentum — the most consistently negative calendar month in Bitcoin's trading history.
LTH Accumulation/DistributionEvery cycle, no exceptions
Long-term holders reliably accumulate through bear markets and distribute into euphoric tops — visible on-chain every single cycle, the most consistent behavioral pattern Bitcoin has.

Separate from the identity speculation below: these are the documented, verifiable facts about Satoshi's activity — from the whitepaper to the final message. No theory, no candidate — just the record.

The Genesis Block Message — January 3, 2009, 18:15:05 UTC
"The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks."
A real headline from The Times (UK), published that exact day, referencing then-Chancellor of the Exchequer Alistair Darling amid the 2008-09 banking crisis. Embedded in Block 0 to serve two purposes: a cryptographic timestamp proving the block wasn't pre-mined earlier, and a pointed critique of fractional-reserve banking and bank bailouts — Bitcoin's very first message was a political statement.

Satoshi Nakamoto — The Suspect List (Still Unsolved)

Three major investigations (HBO documentary, New York Times, feature film) between October 2024 and April 2026 each named a different suspect. Zero definitive proof in any case. This matters for price: Satoshi's dormant ~1.1M BTC (~22,000 addresses, ~$85.5B at April 2026 prices) is the single largest possible market shock if it ever moves — Polymarket priced only a 9% chance of movement in 2026, Kalshi 8%.
